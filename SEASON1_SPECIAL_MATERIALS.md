# PixelRPG Season 1 — Special Materials

Special materials remain a small layer on top of vanilla resources. Vanilla materials stay useful and are not globally replaced.

## World relics
- Archivfragment — uncommon, old-world archive locations.
- Festungssiegel — rare, ruined military sites.
- Sternenlinse — epic, observatory content.
- Siegelbruchstück — legendary, Stronghold/End-seal content.

## Nether materials
- Netherkern-Splitter — rare, early Nether expeditions.
- Karmesinfragment — epic, advanced Nether content.
- Seelenrest — epic, soul-themed high-level encounters.

## Dungeon materials
- Bergkern — uncommon, Verlassene Mine.
- Ascheessenz — rare, Archiv der Asche.
- Festungssplitter — rare, Versunkene Festung.
- Weltenfragment — legendary, Weltenbruch.

## Worldboss materials

The existing boss reward item system remains authoritative for boss-specific named rewards. High-end recipes now consume selected boss materials, including:
- Echoherz
- Netherkern
- Gebundene Enderperle
- Endriss
- Weltenherz
- Horn des Berges

## Rare gathering materials

Rare gathering materials should be implemented as controlled additions to existing gathering loops rather than replacing normal drops. They are intended for profession-specific high-tier recipes.

## Story items

Story items are progression markers and should normally be:
- unique,
- non-tradeable unless explicitly required,
- protected from accidental consumption,
- persisted through the existing item/PDC infrastructure.

## Meisterbrief

Meisterbrief and signed Meisterbrief are already implemented through the existing NPC progression flow and remain the canonical Season-1 profession certification items.

## Drop-rate policy

Exact drop rates are configuration data and require live-server telemetry before final balancing. The material identities and recipe hooks are defined here; final live probabilities remain deployment validation.
