# Season 1 Character-Level Spending Decision

Season 1 keeps character level as a permanent progression value.

## Rule

Character level is not spent, reduced or converted into another resource during Season 1.

- Level 1–60 remains permanent.
- Death never removes character levels.
- Character XP is hard-capped at the level-60 threshold.
- No Season-1 system consumes character levels or XP.
- Profession levels and city specialization are separate progression currencies.

## Reason

This avoids a hidden second economy around character XP while the first season's level curve is being measured. It also keeps the level-60 endpoint stable for balancing.

## Future-season extension point

A later season may introduce a post-cap progression resource. If that happens, it must be a new persisted resource rather than reducing the character's permanent level.

No exact spending cost exists in Season 1 because there is no level-spending mechanic in the Season-1 ruleset.
